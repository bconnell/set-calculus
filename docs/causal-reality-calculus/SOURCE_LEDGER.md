### Provenance rule

Source-derived CRC laws are tagged with:

```text
Source: SRC-CTE-V6-20260909
```

A source-derived law may be generalized into CRC language, but its origin remains recorded here and in the law itself.

Generalization is not treated as proof that the source asserted the generalized CRC form verbatim.

```text
FORMAL-CLOSED != EMPIRICALLY-CALIBRATED
```
