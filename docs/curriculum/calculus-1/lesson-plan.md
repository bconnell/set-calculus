## Embedded Resolvers

### Identity Resolver

Operational question:

> What remains the same?

```text
R_I(x) → invariant identity / relationship
```

### Monad Resolver

Operational question:

> What singular resolution represents this state?

```text
R_M(S) → singular resolved representation
```

The Monad Resolver preserves the distinction:

```text
Potential != Resolved
```
