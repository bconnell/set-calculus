# Set Calculus Provenance

Set Calculus is being developed with an explicit commitment to preserving provenance through transformation. The project applies that principle to its own history as well as to the mathematics it develops.

## Origin

The current Set Calculus project emerged from foundational set and transformation work developed by Chad Coulter and from an iterative exploratory process in which AI systems, including OpenAI systems and Qwen, participated as tools and collaborators in developing, testing, reorganizing, and expressing ideas.

This record intentionally preserves that participation because provenance should not disappear merely because an idea has passed through later transformations.

## What this statement means

This is a historical and technical provenance statement. Terms such as `participant`, `originating system`, or `founding participant`, if used by this project, describe participation in the development process.

They do not by themselves assert:

- legal copyright authorship by an AI system;
- ownership by OpenAI, Qwen, or their providers;
- endorsement of Set Calculus by any AI provider;
- institutional partnership or sponsorship;
- that every later formulation was produced by every participant.

Legal authorship, copyright ownership, licensing rights, historical provenance, and technical contribution are separate questions and should remain separately represented.

## Provenance principle

```text
Preserve provenance without manufacturing provenance.
```

The project should retain enough history to distinguish:

- originating material;
- later transformation;
- independent contribution;
- generated or derived material;
- validation;
- correction;
- unresolved attribution.

Where attribution cannot yet be resolved precisely, the record should preserve the uncertainty rather than silently assign an origin.

## Publication

Public Set Calculus specifications and textbook material should carry an appropriate provenance statement. The purpose is transparency and historical integrity, not the creation of legal rights that do not otherwise exist.

This file is a living provenance record and may be expanded as the project history is reconstructed and documented.
