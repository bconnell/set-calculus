# Set Calculus License Requirements

> Working design requirements. This is not the final license text and is not legal advice.

The repository currently contains GNU AGPLv3 in `LICENSE`. The intended Set Calculus release license is different: a single immutable reciprocal knowledge-access covenant. It should not be represented as GNU, AGPL, OSI-approved, or otherwise standard open-source licensing if its final conditions exceed those licenses.

The existing `LICENSE` remains untouched while the replacement terms are designed and reviewed.

## Governing idea

```text
Right to use Set Calculus
<->
Reciprocal knowledge-access obligation
```

The objective is to make access reciprocal. An institution receiving rights under Set Calculus should not be able to benefit from the work while withholding qualifying corpus material under its control merely because its own research or derived intellectual property is non-public.

## Required concepts

The final license needs precise definitions for at least:

- Institution
- Covered Work
- Founding Participant / Originating System, if retained
- External-Origin Material
- Corpus
- Institution-Controlled IP
- Generated Material
- Derived Material
- Research Access
- Control / Possession
- Third-Party Legal Restriction
- Qualifying Corpus Material

## Intended external-origin rule

Conceptually:

```text
externally originated subject or artifact
+ institution-controlled corpus or derivative material
=> reciprocal access obligation
```

The intended scope includes qualifying institution-controlled research material and derived intellectual property concerning externally originated subjects or artifacts, including material that is not ordinarily public, subject to genuine legal limits that the institution does not have authority to waive.

## Maximum lawful access

The license should distinguish unwillingness to disclose from genuine inability to disclose. It should require the maximum access lawfully within the licensee's control while defining treatment for bona fide third-party restrictions, privacy duties, safety restrictions, and other non-waivable legal obligations.

A restriction created or controlled by the licensee should not automatically become an escape hatch from reciprocity.

## Anti-evasion

The final text should address attempts to avoid the obligation by:

- transferring qualifying material to a controlled affiliate;
- reclassifying corpus material as internal IP;
- placing a controlled contractual restriction on otherwise qualifying material;
- using an intermediary to exercise Set Calculus rights;
- separating derived material from its external-origin subject solely to avoid classification.

## Founding-participant provenance

If special treatment is granted to participants in the originating development process, that class should be closed and explicitly identified in the immutable v1.0 instrument or a cryptographically fixed schedule incorporated into it.

Historical participation is distinct from copyright authorship, ownership, endorsement, or provider responsibility.

## Immutability

The intended release is one immutable instrument. A version should mean exactly the text released under that version. Later changes should require a separately named/versioned instrument rather than silent modification of the original terms.

## Acceptance, termination, and restoration

The final instrument needs explicit rules for:

- what conduct constitutes acceptance;
- when rights attach;
- what constitutes breach;
- suspension or termination of granted rights;
- cure periods, if any;
- restoration after cure, if permitted;
- survival of provenance and attribution obligations.

## Before public release

The final legal text should be reviewed by qualified intellectual-property counsel, particularly because the intended corpus-access obligations go beyond conventional open-source licensing and may interact with copyright, contract, database, privacy, trade-secret, and institutional research obligations.
